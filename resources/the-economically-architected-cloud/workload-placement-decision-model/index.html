Workload Placement Decision Model

A structured model for evaluating where a workload should run based on business requirements, technical fit, migration effort, operating model, risk, and full economic impact.

Step 1

Define the Workload

Start with the workload rather than the cloud provider. Document the business purpose, users, dependencies, data, service levels, regulatory requirements, lifecycle stage, and expected demand pattern.

Step 2

Identify Placement Constraints

Constraint Decision Question
Data Residency Where is the workload and its data permitted to operate?
Latency What response-time or geographic proximity requirements exist?
Security and Compliance What controls, certifications, or isolation requirements apply?
Data Gravity Where do the largest dependent datasets already reside?
Licensing Do software licenses restrict deployment location or platform choice?
Platform Dependency Does the workload depend on services or capabilities available only in certain environments?
Step 3

Compare Placement Options

Dimension What to Evaluate
Technical Fit Performance, scalability, resilience, compatibility, and service maturity.
Migration Effort Application changes, data movement, testing, downtime, tooling, and transition complexity.
Operating Model Skills, support, governance, tooling, automation, security, and platform ownership.
Run Economics Compute, storage, database, network, licensing, support, and managed service costs.
Transition Economics Temporary duplication, migration labor, transfer costs, consulting, retraining, and parallel operations.
Commercial Fit Discounts, commitments, credits, contracts, incentives, and existing enterprise agreements.
Risk Operational risk, vendor concentration, exit cost, technical debt, compliance exposure, and lock-in.
Step 4

Use the Three-State Economic View

Evaluate placement using three financial states:

As-Is: the current cost and operating model.

Transition: temporary costs created during migration, modernization, coexistence, data movement, retraining, and parallel operations.

Target: the expected steady-state cost and operating model after the workload reaches its intended architecture.

Step 5

Document the Decision

Decision Field Required Evidence
Recommended Placement Selected environment and architecture pattern.
Business Rationale Why the placement supports business requirements and expected outcomes.
Economic Rationale As-Is, Transition, and Target cost implications.
Key Assumptions Demand, pricing, migration effort, commitments, growth, and operational assumptions.
Risks Material financial, technical, operational, regulatory, and commercial risks.
Review Trigger Conditions requiring the placement decision to be reassessed.
